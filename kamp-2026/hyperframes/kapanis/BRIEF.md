# Kapanış: Ankara kampı brand lockup (CAN'LI 7/24 Eğitim Merkezi)

**Category:** `logo-reveal` (form category, no search; user-supplied vector logo).

**Approved by the user upfront** (design + render). Ask no questions; render once lint/check/snapshots pass.

## Context

Client: CAN'LI 7/24 Eğitim Merkezi (Turkish customs-exam prep school). The promo is a venue walkthrough of the Ankara camp
(starts 8 November, New Park Otel, Ankara, halls on the 8th floor). The narration ends right before this sting with
"Ankara kamp kayıtlarımız an itibariyle dolmuştur. Herkesin bilgilerine." This sting has no voice. It is full-frame and
opaque and opens with a quick flash-in from the master.

## Beats

- 0.00–1.30 logo assembles centred in the upper half: badge "7" and "24" pop, CAN'LI rises letter by letter, the
  graduation cap drops onto the A with a small bounce, the "Eğitim Merkezi" tagline wipes in. Same choreography as
  `tebrik-2026/hyperframes/kapanis-logo`, tighter.
- 1.40 "ANKARA KAMPI" label: tracking-in, orange underline draws.
- 1.80 hero date: "08 KASIM" large and heavy (punch with overshoot), "’DA BAŞLIYOR" as the second line.
- 2.60 info line "NEW PARK OTEL · 8. KAT · ANKARA" with a map-pin icon.
- 3.20 rubber stamp "KAYITLAR DOLDU" slams in: rotated about -8°, scale 1.6→1, tiny shake, ink-distressed edge,
  red-orange #E8432E. It must read clearly at phone size.
- 3.40–6.00 hold the final lockup alive: slow glow drift, one light sweep across the logo at about 4.2 s.
  There is no fade to black, and the last frame is a clean, complete lockup.

## Look

Brand colours: orange #F16D25, anthracite #414141, navy #070B1F, navy2 #121A3D, white #FFFFFF, stamp ink #E8432E.
The background is a deep navy gradient with a warm orange radial glow and a subtle LED pixel grid and scanline texture,
for a premium TV-broadcast feel. Montserrat variable (100–900) comes from local woff2 files split latin / latin-ext by
unicode-range, so İ ı Ş ş Ğ ğ Ç Ö Ü render. Turkish uppercase is written literally, never with CSS text-transform.
Side safe margin is about 90 px, and everything stays above y = 1720.
